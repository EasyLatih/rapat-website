Career initiatives should create more than attendance numbers.

Organisations may attract many participants, but understanding the full journey from registration to actual employment outcome can remain challenging.

01

Fragmented data

Jobseeker information, employer vacancies and follow-up outcomes often sit in separate files or systems.

02

Weak post-event visibility

Attendance may be known, while interviews, offers, placements and unresolved skills gaps are harder to track.

03

Harder to prove impact

Without consistent outcome data, it is difficult to show which initiatives generate the strongest employment impact.

One simple journey from registration to placement.

RAPAT is designed as a digital layer around career initiatives, not another complicated portal.

STEP 1

Register

Jobseekers create a profile.

STEP 2

Match

Profiles connect with relevant vacancies and opportunities.

STEP 3

Engage

Employers review candidates and conduct interviews or follow-up.

STEP 4

Track

RAPAT records progress from application to interview and offer.

STEP 5

Report

Partners gain a clearer picture of employment outcomes and gaps.
