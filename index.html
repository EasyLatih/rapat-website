Career events should create more than attendance numbers.

Many initiatives succeed in attracting participants, but agencies often struggle to see the full journey from registration to actual employment outcome.

01

Fragmented data

Jobseeker information, employer vacancies and follow-up outcomes often sit in separate files or systems.

02

Weak post-event visibility

Attendance may be known, but interviews, offers, placements and unresolved skills gaps can be harder to track.

03

Harder to prove ROI

Without consistent outcome data, it is difficult to show which initiatives generate the strongest employment impact.

One simple journey from registration to placement.

RAPAT is designed as the digital layer around career initiatives, not as another complicated portal.

STEP 1

Register

Jobseekers create a profile before or during the event.

STEP 2

Match

Profiles are connected with relevant vacancies and opportunities.

STEP 3

Engage

Employers review candidates and conduct interviews or follow-up.

STEP 4

Track

RAPAT records the movement from application to interview and offer.

STEP 5

Report

Agencies receive a clearer picture of employment outcomes and gaps.
